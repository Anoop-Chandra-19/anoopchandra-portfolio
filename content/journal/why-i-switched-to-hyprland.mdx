i3 is wonderful. It's also from 2009. Animations are nonexistent, the rendering pipeline is X11, and HiDPI fractional scaling is a polite fiction.

Hyprland is a Wayland compositor with the same tiling DNA — but it animates, it composites, it handles 1.5x scaling on a 4K display without me writing any xrandr scripts.

## What actually changed

- Window animations that aren't distracting — bezier curves, ~150ms
- Real fractional scaling, finally
- Per-monitor workspaces that just work
- A config file that reads like a config file, not a manifesto

<Callout color="teal">If you've been on i3/sway and stayed away from Hyprland because of the drama — try it. The compositor is excellent.</Callout>

## What I miss

Stability, mostly. i3 never crashed. Hyprland crashes maybe once a week. I'll take it for what I get back.
